Recommended Actions by Risk Level
🔴 HIGH RISK (61–100)
Reduce volume 20–30% immediately. No max-effort lifts or full-speed running. Daily monitoring required. Notify athletic trainer. Reassess in 48 hours before returning to full practice.
🟡 MODERATE RISK (31–60)
Monitor closely. Reduce intensity by 10–15% if subjective wellness score is low. Flag for daily check-in with S&C staff. Re-evaluate after next GPS session. Consider reducing contact exposure.
🟢 LOW RISK (0–30)
Full training — continue monitoring. Maintain current load progression. Ensure adequate recovery between high-intensity days. No restrictions.
